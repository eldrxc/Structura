Press Validate structure to check your molecule and see its IUPAC name.

Names support branched chains and single carbon rings: alkyl branches, halogens, ethers, alcohols, thiols, amines, aldehydes, ketones, acids, amides, nitriles and double/triple bonds. Esters (e.g. methyl ethanoate), secondary/tertiary amines (e.g. N-methylethanamine) and N-substituted amides are named too. Fused rings and other unusual arrangements are refused rather than guessed.
